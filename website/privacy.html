Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version: Trail records which of your chosen apps you used each day and for how many minutes. That's it. It never records keystrokes, screenshots, window titles, files, or websites. Your data is yours: you can export it or delete it at any time.

Who we are

Trail is a free, open-source desktop app published by Ziggiphase (github.com/Ziggiphase/trail). In this policy, "Trail", "we" and "us" mean the publisher of the app; "you" means a person using it.

What Trail collects

DataWhyWhere it's kept
Usage totals: the program name of each app you chose to track (for example code), the date, and the number of seconds you used it that day. To show your history, goal, streaks and charts. On your PC until synced; then in your account.
Computer name: the name of each PC you use Trail on (you can change it in Settings). So totals from several computers can be combined.Your account.
Account details: your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google also shares your name and profile picture.To sign you in and keep your data separate from everyone else's. Your account.
Settings: your tracked apps, goal and preferences.To make the app work your way. Only on your PC.

What Trail never collects

  • Keystrokes, mouse movements, screenshots or screen recordings.
  • Window titles, file names, documents or anything you type. (Trail reads the title of the window in front only to check a rule you created, such as "count Chrome only on Colab", and immediately discards it.)
  • Websites you visit, or apps you didn't choose to track.
  • Advertising IDs or tracking cookies. Trail has no ads and doesn't sell or share data.

Using Trail without an account

If you choose "Continue without an account", nothing is sent to our servers. Your time stays on your PC.

Services we rely on

These companies process data on our behalf, only to run Trail:

  • Supabase: hosts the database and the sign-in system that store your account and usage totals.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm who you are.
  • Our email provider: sends sign-in emails when you sign in with email.
  • GitHub: hosts the downloads and this website. When Trail checks for updates it contacts GitHub, which can see your IP address like any website.

How your data is protected

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Database rules ensure each account can only ever read or change its own data.
  • On Windows, your sign-in token is encrypted so only your Windows user account can use it.

How long we keep it

We keep your usage totals for as long as you have an account. You can delete them yourself at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Settings → Your data → Export to CSV.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Your data → Delete my cloud data. It's removed immediately.
  • Delete your account: contact us (below) and we'll delete your account and all its data.
  • Stop tracking: pause Trail, switch apps off, sign out, or uninstall it at any time.

Depending on where you live, laws such as Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA) or the EU/UK GDPR give you rights to access, correct, delete and move your data, and to object to how it's used. We process your data to provide the service you asked for. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Trail isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Ziggiphase/trail/issues. For private requests (like deleting your account), write "privacy request" in the title and we'll reply with a private way to reach us.
